Academic Streams

Admission Open For

Choose your pathway — whether in Science, Commerce, Humanities, or Islamic Studies, every stream is tailored to balance academic brilliance with strong moral and spiritual growth.

Science Stream

Higher Secondary – Science

Hamda Science Academy offers Higher Secondary Science education through an integrated academic model that combines the regular curriculum with focused entrance-examination preparation. Students can choose from Bio Science, Computer Science and Psychology Science options. Along with their Higher Secondary studies, students receive integrated coaching for NEET, KEAM, JEE and CUET, helping them build a strong academic foundation and prepare systematically for higher education and competitive entrance examinations.

Explore Science Stream
Commerce Stream

Higher Secondary – Commerce

Our Higher Secondary Commerce programme is designed for students who wish to pursue careers in commerce, finance, professional courses and competitive examinations. Along with Commerce with Computer Application, students receive integrated preparation for CA Foundation, CMA Foundation and CUET, with additional foundation-level training for PSC and UPSC examinations. The programme aims to develop strong conceptual knowledge, practical skills and career awareness from the higher secondary level itself.

Explore Commerce Stream
Humanities Stream

Higher Secondary – Humanities

The Higher Secondary Humanities programme at Hamda Science Academy is specially designed for students aspiring towards government services, competitive examinations and higher studies. The programme combines the Humanities curriculum with structured preparation for PSC, UPSC and CUET, enabling students to develop a strong understanding of social sciences while gradually building the knowledge and skills required for competitive examinations and future academic pursuits.

Explore Humanities Stream
Islamic Studies & Hadiya

School of Islamic Studies

Hadiya + Commerce

The School of Islamic Studies provides an integrated educational environment where Islamic learning and Higher Secondary education are pursued together. Through the Hadiya Higher Secondary programme under Markaz, Calicut, students can pursue Higher Secondary Commerce along with systematic Islamic studies. The programme focuses on nurturing students with academic knowledge, religious understanding, discipline, moral values and a balanced approach to education.
