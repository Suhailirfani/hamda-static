About Us

Education Beyond Academics

Hamda International Campus is a girls-only integrated educational campus committed to nurturing confident, knowledgeable, and value-driven young women.

Our educational approach combines higher secondary education across Science, Commerce, and Humanities, entrance-exam preparation (NEET, JEE, CA Foundation, Civil Services), Islamic learning, and character development within a supportive residential environment.

We aim to provide students with the knowledge, skills, and values required to succeed in higher education, competitive examinations, and their future careers. Managed with pride by Markazuda'wa Thil Islamiyya Thazhapra.

Our Vision

To be a premier national benchmark in female-only residential education across Science, Commerce, and Humanities.

Our Mission

Delivering expert integrated curricula, nurturing moral integrity, and offering a secure, peaceful learning sanctuary.

Hostel & Living

A Secure Residential Oasis

We understand that academic productivity is profoundly impacted by a student's living environment. Our campus provides a premium, fully residential **Girls-Only Hostel** designed to be a comfortable "home away from home."

24/7 Monitored Surveillance: Complete boundary walling, secure access controls, and active supervision guarantee total security.
Nutritious Dining: Serving fresh, clean, and nutritious vegetarian and non-vegetarian food customized to optimize physical and cognitive health.
Dedicated Female Staff: Wardens, medical assistants, kitchen workers, and security supervisors are fully represented by female personnel, ensuring absolute comfort.
Spiritual and Moral Grounding: Regular ethical sessions, spiritual classes, and moral guidelines cultivate character and empathy.
