Our Academic Programs

Comprehensive Curriculum

Plus One & Plus Two in Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams, seamlessly integrated with professional coaching, career readiness, and Islamic values.

Plus One & Plus Two Science

Our flagship Science stream delivers deep conceptual clarity in physical and life sciences with NCERT board mastery and simultaneous intensive preparation for NEET (Medical) and JEE (Engineering).

Theoretical learning is reinforced through hands-on laboratory experiments, daily MCQ worksheets, negative-marking tests, and personalized mentoring by premier educators.

Biology Science Computer Science Psychology Science

Plus One & Plus Two Commerce

A robust, career-focused Commerce stream designed for female students aiming for excellence in finance, corporate leadership, banking, and entrepreneurship.

Includes dedicated foundation training for professional certifications such as CA Foundation (Chartered Accountancy), CMA, and CS alongside practical financial literacy and case study workshops.

Commerce With Political Science Computer Commerce
Apply for Commerce

Plus One & Plus Two Humanities

An inspiring, intellectually vibrant Humanities stream cultivating deep analytical skills, historical understanding, governance insights, and global perspectives.

Integrated with early orientation for UPSC / Civil Services, CUET (Central Universities), Law Entrance (CLAT), and journalism, empowering women to excel in public service and academic scholarship.

Academic Excellence & Competitive Edge

Integrated Training for Competitive Exams

Integrated seamlessly alongside the regular Plus One & Plus Two curriculum, empowering students to crack state and national level examinations with high confidence.

NEET, JEE, KEAM

Rigorous coaching for medical and engineering admissions with daily MCQs, OMR tests, numerical solving, and NCERT mastery.

CA, CMA Foundation

Foundation mentoring for Chartered Accountancy & Cost Management, covering financial accounting, business laws, and quantitative aptitude.

PSC, UPSC

Structured early orientation for Civil Services (UPSC) and Kerala PSC, developing strong general studies, Indian polity, and current affairs analysis.

KLEE, CLAT

Focused training for national and state law entrance examinations (CLAT & KLEE) with legal aptitude, logical reasoning, and English comprehension.
